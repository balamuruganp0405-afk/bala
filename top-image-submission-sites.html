50+ Image Submission Sites in 2024

Published: May 14, 2026
Read Time: 6 min read
Author: Balamurugan P

In the constantly changing world of making websites show up on Google, it’s super important to stay ahead to do well online. We all know using the right words and creating good content is important, but there’s this cool trick not many people talk about – it’s called image submission. In this guide, we’ll talk about why putting up pictures is so important for Google and give you a big list of Image Submission Sites where you can do it to make your website even better.

Understanding Image Submission in SEO:

Image submission means sharing relevant pictures on different websites to make more people see your website. Search engines really like pictures, and if you set them up right, they can help your website show up more on Google. Here’s why it’s a good idea:

Enhanced Visibility:

  • Google looks at pictures and makes them easy to find, so more people can see your website.
  • Many people search for pictures, so having good ones can bring in users who like visual stuff.

Backlink Opportunities:

  • Putting your pictures on different websites can get you more links, which makes your website more important.
  • Make sure the links lead back to your website to get the most out of it.

Improved User Engagement:

  • Pictures are interesting, so if your website has cool pictures, people will spend more time looking at it.
  • Good pictures also make people want to share your website on social media.

Image Submission Sites in 2024 :

Now that we understand the importance of image submission, let’s explore a list of websites where you can submit your images to amplify your SEO efforts:

Flickr:

A widely used platform for image sharing, Flickr allows you to showcase your photos and link back to your website.

Instagram:

Leverage the power of Instagram by sharing visually appealing images and using relevant hashtags to reach a broader audience.

Pinterest:

Ideal for businesses with visually appealing products, Pinterest enables you to create boards and drive traffic through pinned images.

Imgur:

Known for its user-friendly interface, Imgur is a popular image hosting platform where you can submit images and gain exposure.

DeviantArt:

A community of artists and art enthusiasts, DeviantArt is an excellent platform to showcase your creative work and link to your website.

500px:

Targeting photography enthusiasts, 500px allows you to submit high-quality images and connect with a community of like-minded individuals.

FreeImages:

As the name suggests, FreeImages is a platform where you can share your images for free and include a link to your website.

Shutterstock:

While primarily a stock photo platform, Shutterstock allows contributors to showcase their work and gain exposure.

Pixabay:

A repository of high-quality images, Pixabay allows you to contribute your visuals and include links to your website in the image descriptions.

Frequently Asked Questions

What are image submission sites?

Platforms like Pinterest, Flickr, and Pixabay where you can upload images, infographics, and photos with a source link back to your website.

How does image submission improve search traffic?

Optimized images can rank in Google Image Search, bringing in visual search traffic. They also build backlinks and social signals.

What is image alt text?

Alt text (alternative text) describes the contents of an image to search engines and screen readers, helping search engines understand and rank the image.
