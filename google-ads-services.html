Frequently Asked Questions

What is the recommended daily budget for Google Ads?

It depends on keyword cost-per-click (CPC) in your industry, but I recommend a starting budget of $10 to $20 per day ($300 - $600 per month) to collect sufficient traffic data to optimize bids.

How fast do Google Ads start working?

Google Ads campaigns start running immediately once the search engine reviews and approves them. You can expect qualified traffic and direct phone calls or lead submissions within the first 24 to 48 hours.

How do you make sure my budget is not wasted?

By starting with highly targeted exact/phrase match keywords and building a robust list of pre-configured negative keywords. I inspect actual search term logs daily to exclude unqualified queries.
