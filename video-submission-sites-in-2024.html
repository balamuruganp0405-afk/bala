50+ Video Submission Sites in 2024

Published: May 18, 2026
Read Time: 6 min read
Author: Balamurugan P

Video Submission Sites in 2024 – In the ever-changing world of Search Engine Optimization (SEO), it’s important to stay ahead. Video submission is now a powerful tool in SEO, giving a special way to increase how easily people can find you online. This guide explains why video submission is important and gives you a list of specific websites to make the most of your SEO work.

Video Submission in SEO

In today’s online world filled with pictures and videos, videos are really important for keeping people interested. Search engines like Google really like it when websites have different kinds of content, and adding videos can make your website more interesting. Putting up videos not only makes your website more enjoyable for visitors but also helps a lot with getting your site to show up in search results.

Increased Visibility:

Videos have the potential to capture attention quickly, making them more shareable and engaging. Platforms like YouTube, Vimeo, and Dailymotion act as search engines in their right, amplifying your content’s reach beyond traditional search results.

Quality Backlinks:

Video submission on reputable platforms generates valuable backlinks to your website. Search engines perceive these backlinks as a vote of confidence, boosting your site’s authority and ranking.

Enhanced User Experience:

Videos provide a dynamic and interactive user experience, reducing bounce rates and increasing the time visitors spend on your site. Search engines recognize this positive engagement, contributing to improved rankings.

Social Media Amplification:

Videos are highly shareable on social media platforms, creating a ripple effect across various channels. This social amplification not only extends your content’s reach but also signals search engines about its relevance and popularity.

Video Submission Sites in 2024:

Youtube

As the second-largest search engine globally, YouTube is a must for video submission. Create a dedicated channel, optimize video titles and descriptions with relevant keywords, and engage with your audience through comments.

Vimeo

Known for its high-quality content, Vimeo attracts a more discerning audience. Leverage Vimeo for professional and polished videos, optimizing your profile with accurate information and links.

Dailymotion

With a user-friendly interface, Dailymotion is an excellent platform for reaching a diverse audience. Ensure your videos are tagged appropriately, and include a link back to your website in the description.

Facebook

Capitalize on Facebook’s massive user base by uploading videos directly to the platform. Craft compelling captions, use relevant hashtags, and encourage sharing to maximize visibility.

Instagram

Instagram’s visual appeal makes it ideal for short, engaging videos. Leverage IGTV for longer content, and don’t forget to optimize your video captions and hashtags.

Wistia

Wistia is a platform tailored for business video hosting. Use it for marketing videos, webinars, and tutorials. The platform provides analytics to track video performance.

Metacafe

While not as mainstream as YouTube, Metacafe offers a platform for viral video content. Focus on creating attention-grabbing videos to increase visibility.

Veoh

Veoh allows users to upload, share, and discover videos. Optimize your profile, and use descriptive titles and tags to improve searchability.

Twitch

Ideal for live streaming and gaming content, Twitch can be a unique addition to your video submission strategy. Engage with your audience through live chat for added visibility.

TED Talks

If your content aligns with the TED Talks format, consider submitting your videos to this prestigious platform. TED Talks attract a global audience interested in insightful and educational content.
