Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common queries about digital strategy, project timelines, and pricing models.

What digital marketing services do you specialize in?

I specialize in Search Engine Optimization (SEO) to drive organic visibility, Search Engine Marketing (Google Ads / PPC) to capture high-intent leads instantly, and Web Design & Development (using clean HTML, CSS, JS) to build responsive, conversion-optimized marketing assets.

How long does it take to see organic SEO results?

Generally, SEO is a long-term investment. While some technical improvements can result in quick indexing boosts, comprehensive organic growth (ranking on page 1 for high-value competitive keywords) typically takes between 3 to 6 months depending on your site's history and niche competition.

How do you measure and report Google Ads success?

We measure success based on your primary business objectives: Cost Per Acquisition (CPA), Return On Ad Spend (ROAS), Lead Conversion Rate, and overall Lead Volume. I set up tracking through Google Tag Manager and provide real-time reporting dashboards showing exact metrics.

Do you design websites from scratch or use platforms?

I build fully custom responsive HTML/CSS/JS layouts for lightning-fast speeds and technical search engine compliance, and I also work with CMS frameworks like WordPress to allow client editors to publish blog content easily without needing coding skills.

What are your billing rates and structures?

I offer project-based structures for one-off assets (like website redesigns), hourly consultation, and monthly retainer arrangements for ongoing marketing strategies (like SEO optimization or search ads management). Every proposal is structured to meet your specific budget targets.
