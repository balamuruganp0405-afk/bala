Professional Journey

My career in digital technology began after completing my **Bachelor of Engineering (Mechanical Engineering) in 2022** from Velammal College of Engineering and Technology. During my engineering studies, I developed a strong interest in analytical problem-solving and marketing mechanics, which naturally pulled me toward search engine algorithms and data-driven marketing.

I started my professional path as an **SEO Analyst**, learning search engines, site crawlability patterns, and user intent triggers. Demonstrating structured project execution, I progressed rapidly into **SEO Executive** roles, taking complete charge of campaign architecture, technical audits, and performance marketing budgets.

Over the past 4 years, I have successfully managed international campaigns for clients across the **USA, UAE, India, Australia, and Ireland**. I am intensely passionate about helping companies build clean organic search pipelines, scale Google and Meta Ads ROAS, optimize landing page layouts, and leverage Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) strategies to secure maximum visibility in AI search.
