Freelance SEO Specialist & Digital Marketer

From being a Mechanical Engineering graduate to becoming a Digital Marketing Professional, my journey proves that passion, dedication, and consistent learning can open new opportunities and lead to meaningful success.

My name is Balamurugan P, and I am a full-time Freelance SEO Specialist and Digital Marketer. I completed my Bachelor of Engineering (Mechanical Engineering) in 2022 from Velammal College of Engineering and Technology.

During my college years, I developed a strong interest in digital technology and online marketing. Although my academic background was in Mechanical Engineering, I discovered my passion for SEO and digital marketing. This motivated me to learn search engine optimization, lead generation, content marketing, website optimization, and paid advertising.

After graduation, I dedicated myself to gaining hands-on experience through real-world projects and continuous learning. Over time, I built expertise in helping businesses improve their online visibility, increase website traffic, generate quality leads, and achieve business growth through effective digital marketing strategies.

I have successfully handled 50+ projects across various industries, including Healthcare, IT, Software, Manufacturing, Consultancy, and Local Businesses. Having worked with clients from the USA, UAE, India, Australia, and Ireland, this international exposure has helped me understand different markets, customer behaviors, and business objectives, enabling me to create result-driven digital marketing strategies.
